Privacy Policy for The Dark Shell

Last updated: November 2026

The Dark Shell respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled by our automated content publishing tool.

1. Data Collection & Usage

This application is used strictly to publish short-form video content to our official TikTok channel (https://www.tiktok.com/@thedarkshell). We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal data from third-party TikTok users.

2. Authentication Tokens

OAuth access tokens generated via TikTok Login Kit are used solely to authorize video uploads to our own account and are stored locally in a secure environment.

3. Contact

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, reach out via our official TikTok profile: https://www.tiktok.com/@thedarkshell
